# Contributing to FigLab

FigLab is developed with Node.js 24 and pnpm 10. Run `corepack enable`, `pnpm install`,
`pnpm check`, `pnpm typecheck`, and `pnpm test:unit` before submitting a change.

Behavior changes follow test-driven development: add a focused failing test, confirm the expected
failure, implement the smallest change, and rerun the focused and full relevant suites.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only.
